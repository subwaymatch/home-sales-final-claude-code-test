How the numbers are computed

  • Valid price means sale_price from $10,000 to $5,000,000, inclusive. Every question uses valid prices, except Q2 and Q3 (all rows), and Q9 (all rows with a sale date).
  • City means city and state together, such as "Champaign, IL". Campus area means the university column.
  • Days on market is contract_date minus list_date, in days, using only sales that have both dates. A blank date is not zero.
  • Year and month come from sale_date.
  • Median is used unless a question asks for a mean. When two or more cities or campus areas tie, every tied one is named.
  • Rounding: dollars to whole dollars; days, miles and percents to one decimal place.
  • Repeated street addresses are kept. listing_id is the key, and parcel_number tells units in one building apart.

Checking the numbers

The page computes Q1 to Q6 in the browser. scripts/check_q1_q6.py computes the same six answers a second way, with pandas, from the same CSV file. Run it from the repository root with python3 scripts/check_q1_q6.py. The script prints its results and does not write any file.
